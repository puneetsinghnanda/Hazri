BANIS
The nine banis in Hazri
hazri.app · 7 min read · updated 12 November
Hazri ships nine banis. Four of them are in Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji and five are not, which is why the meanings that can be shown differ from one to the next.
Every line comes from a reviewed source file and is built into the app before release. Nothing is translated by a machine, and where a source has no entry the app shows a gap rather than filling it.
Written and reviewed on hazri.app. The app shows it as written and does not summarise it.
