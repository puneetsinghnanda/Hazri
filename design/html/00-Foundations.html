Ang Sang — foundations
Phase 1 design system: principles, colour, type, components and icons. Colours are shown light / dark.
Principles
Gurbani first
No ads, pop-ups or promotions on any Gurbani screen. Gurbani is never cut off mid-line.
Always with you
Everything works offline. No login. Your diary stays on your phone.
Follow the paath
The line being recited is highlighted and kept in the upper third of the screen.
Gentle, not gamified
Streaks forgive missed days. Badges (Phase 3) mark milestones — never points for Gurbani.
Three languages, equal
Every screen in ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, हिन्दी and English; bani in Gurmukhi, Devanagari or Roman.
Easy for elders
Touch targets at least 48dp; elder mode scales text to 150% and buttons to 64dp.
