Joti Jot of Sri Guru Harkrishan Sahib Ji
Thursday, 1 April · 2 min read
The eighth Guru, still a child, completed his life on earth in Delhi in 1664.
Serving the sick
That year smallpox spread through Delhi. The Guru went among the sick of every faith, giving them water from the well at the bungalow where he stayed.
