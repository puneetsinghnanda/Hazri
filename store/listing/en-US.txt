APP NAME (max 30)
Hazri: Nitnem & Gurbani Path

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Nitnem Gurbani with audio: Japji Sahib, Sukhmani Sahib, Simran, Gurpurab dates

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Hazri is a Nitnem and Gurbani app for your daily Sikh path. Read and listen to Japji Sahib, Jaap Sahib, Rehras Sahib, Kirtan Sohila and Sukhmani Sahib with audio, follow the Nanakshahi calendar of Gurpurab dates, sit in Waheguru Simran and do path together with your family and sangat. Mark your Hazri to Waheguru, every day.

No ads. No login. No password. Your Nitnem gutka, the Gurbani audio, the calendar and your diary are all on your phone and work offline.

NITNEM GURBANI WITH AUDIO
Every bani of the daily Nitnem, in Gurmukhi, each with its own recitation:
• Japji Sahib
• Jaap Sahib
• Tav Prasad Savaiye
• Chaupai Sahib
• Anand Sahib
• Rehras Sahib
• Kirtan Sohila (Sohila Sahib)
• Sukhmani Sahib
• Ardas

READ ALONG AS THE PATH IS RECITED
• Each line of Gurbani lights up as it is recited, so you never lose your place
• Gurmukhi with Hindi (Devanagari) and English transliteration, Larivaar and vishraam pause marks
• Gurbani meanings (arth) in English and Punjabi, and in Hindi for the banis from Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji
• Adjustable text size and a dark mode for Amrit Vela
• Gurbani audio keeps playing with the screen off: lock screen and Bluetooth controls, playback speed and a sleep timer

YOUR NITNEM, MORNING TO NIGHT
• The banis of each part of the day: Amrit Vela, Rehras at evening and Kirtan Sohila before sleep
• Set a daily path goal and get a reminder at the time you choose
• An Amrit Vela reminder every morning

NANAKSHAHI CALENDAR AND GURPURAB DATES
• Every Gurpurab of the year: Prakash Purab, Gurgaddi Divas, Joti Jot and Shaheedi Purab
• Sangrand, Masya and Puranmashi dates, Jor Melas and days of Sikh history
• Today's Nanakshahi date, and a reminder on the morning of each Gurpurab
• Stories of the Gurpurabs, historic Gurdwaras and days of Sikh history

WAHEGURU SIMRAN
• Sit in Naam Simran with a gentle Waheguru recitation and a timer
• A live globe shows where the sangat is in Simran right now, city by city. Never by name

SIKH SAAKHIS (SAKHIS)
• Illustrated saakhis from Sikh history and the lives of the Gurus
• Read them or listen, narrated in Punjabi, with a lesson at the end of each

GURMAT QUIZ
• 340 Sikh quiz questions in 17 topics: the ten Gurus, Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, the Five Takhts, historic Gurdwaras, Sikh banis, Sikh heroes, Sikh history and culture
• Five questions at a time, with a new topic each day
• Make a profile to earn points and climb the daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards

DO PATH TOGETHER WITH THE SANGAT
• Join the Sangat groups for Japji Sahib, Sukhmani Sahib and Naam Simran
• Make a private group for your family, Gurdwara or friends, with a shared goal: so many Japji Sahib or Sukhmani Sahib path, or minutes of Naam Simran, by a date you choose
• Watch the goal fill as everyone does their path. Invite with a link or a code

A DIARY OF YOUR PATH
• Every day you marked your Hazri, your streak now and your longest
• Badges for steady practice, earned from your own diary
• Today's Vichar: a line from Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji each day, to read and share
• Optional weekly backup, so a new phone can bring it all back

PUNJABI, HINDI AND ENGLISH
Use Hazri in Punjabi, Hindi or English.

YOUR PRIVACY
Nothing leaves your phone unless you make a profile or join a group, and then only what is needed for them to work. Hazri has no ads, no advertising ID and no tracking, and never sells or shares your data. Read exactly what is sent, and when, at hazri.me/privacy.

Every line of Gurbani in Hazri comes from reviewed sources. Nothing is machine-translated.

Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh.
