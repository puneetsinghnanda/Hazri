APP NAME (max 30)
Hazri: Nitnem, Simran & Sangat

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Nitnem with audio, Simran, Gurpurab calendar and Sangat. No ads, no login.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Hazri is a daily companion for your Sikh practice. Read and hear your Nitnem, sit in Naam Simran, keep a private diary of your paath and do paath together with your family and sangat. Mark your Hazri to Waheguru, every day.

No ads. No login. No password. Reading a bani, hearing it, seeing the calendar and keeping your diary all happen on your phone and work with no internet.

READ ALONG AS THE PAATH IS RECITED
• Japji Sahib, Jaap Sahib, Tav Prasad Savaiye, Chaupai Sahib, Anand Sahib, Rehras Sahib, Kirtan Sohila, Sukhmani Sahib and Ardas, each with its own recitation
• Every line lights up as it is recited, so you never lose your place
• Meanings in English and Punjabi, and in Hindi for the banis from Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji
• Listen with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen

YOUR WHOLE NITNEM, MORNING TO NIGHT
• Each bani in its part of the day: Amrit Vela, evening and before sleep
• Set your daily paath and get a gentle reminder at the time you choose
• An Amrit Vela reminder, and reminders on the morning of Gurpurabs and Sikh days

A DIARY THAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• Every day you marked your Hazri, your streak now and your longest
• Badges for steady practice, earned quietly from your own diary
• Optional weekly backup, so a new phone can bring it all back

SIMRAN WITH THE SANGAT OF THE WORLD
• Sit in Naam Simran with a gentle recitation and a timer
• A live globe shows where the sangat is in Simran right now, city by city. Never by name

PAATH TOGETHER
• Join the Sangat groups, or make a private group for your family and friends
• Set a shared goal and watch it fill as everyone does their paath
• Invite with a link or a code. Only the people you invite can see your group

SAAKHIS FOR EVERY DAY
• Illustrated saakhis from Sikh history to read and listen to, narrated in Punjabi
• A lesson at the end of each one

GURMAT QUIZ
• 340 questions on the Gurus, Gurdwaras, banis, Sikh heroes and history, in 17 topics
• Five questions at a time, with a new topic each day
• Make a profile to earn points and appear on the daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards

THE NANAKSHAHI CALENDAR
• Every Gurpurab and Sikh day of the year, with today's Nanakshahi date
• Stories of the Gurpurabs, Gurdwaras and days of Sikh history
• Today's Vichar: a line from Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji each day, to read and share

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Hazri speaks Punjabi, Hindi and English.

YOUR PRIVACY
Nothing leaves your phone unless you make a profile or join a group, and then only what is needed for them to work. Hazri has no ads, no advertising ID and no tracking, and never sells or shares your data. Read exactly what is sent, and when, at hazri.me/privacy.

Every line of Gurbani in Hazri comes from reviewed sources. Nothing is machine-translated.

Waheguru Ji Ka Khalsa, Waheguru Ji Ki Fateh.
