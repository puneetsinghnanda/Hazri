    The Sangat

    Three groups open to everybody, and your own

    Join the Amrit Vela, Simran or Sukhmani Sahib Sangat, or start a private group for your family or your gurdwara. A group has one goal and an end date, and every paath its members finish counts toward it.

    You join with a link or a code. Nobody needs a password, and nobody sees your diary.

    Gurpurab calendar

    Every Gurpurab of the year, with the story of the day

    Dates follow the SGPC’s Nanakshahi calendar for 558, from 14 March 2026 to 13 March 2027. Hazri holds the whole year on your phone and reminds you on the morning of each day: at 06:00 for a Gurpurab, at 07:00 for other Sikh days.
